# Sprint 5.5: Source of Funds Report

**Priority:** MEDIUM - Blockpit charges €19.99 per report for this. Banks increasingly demand crypto origin proof, especially in DACH.
**Depends on:** Sprint 4.2 (country module), Sprint 5.4 (broad chain coverage helps here)
**Files:** New `src/reports/source_of_funds.py`, new frontend page

---

## Context

German and Austrian banks are increasingly requesting "Herkunftsnachweis" (proof of origin) for large crypto deposits converted to fiat. Users who can't prove where their crypto came from face frozen accounts. Blockpit is the only tool offering this - at €19.99 per report.

---

## Task 5.5.1: Transaction Trail Generator

**Create:** `src/reports/source_of_funds.py`

For a given asset + amount, trace backward through the full transaction history:
1. Current holding: X ETH in wallet 0xABC
2. Received from: bridge from Arbitrum on [date]
3. Before that: swap from USDC on Uniswap on [date]
4. Before that: deposited from Coinbase on [date]
5. Original acquisition: purchased on Coinbase on [date] for $Y

**Output:**
- Complete chain of custody per holding
- Acquisition method per holding (exchange purchase, staking reward, airdrop, DeFi yield)
- Timeline visualization data
- Total acquisition cost

---

## Task 5.5.2: Source of Funds PDF Report

Generate a professional PDF document suitable for bank/exchange KYC submission:
- Header: user name, report date, wallet addresses covered
- Per-holding section: asset, amount, acquisition method, date, original source
- Transaction trail (simplified, not raw tx hashes)
- Total portfolio value and weighted average acquisition cost
- Signature block: "This report was generated by [Product] based on on-chain transaction data"
