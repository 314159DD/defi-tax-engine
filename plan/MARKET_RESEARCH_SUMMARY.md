## Top User Pain Points (Both Markets)

1. **DeFi tracking is broken** - LP positions misclassified, bridges treated as sales, phantom gains
2. **Transaction count inflation** - spam tokens and micro-rewards inflate counts → higher bills
3. **Missing cost basis** - incomplete data = incorrect (usually higher) tax bills
4. **Support quality drops at scale** - complex DeFi situations get no help
5. **Trust gap (DE specific)** - "Will my Finanzamt accept this report?"
6. **Privacy concerns** - uploading complete financial history to cloud servers

## Regulatory Tailwinds

- **1099-DA (US):** Live 2025, cost basis 2026. DeFi/self-custody exempt → software needed indefinitely.
- **DAC8 (EU):** Live 2026, data exchange 2027. Converts non-filers to customers.
- **DeFi broker rule (US):** Repealed early 2026 - DeFi users must self-report.
- **BMF-Schreiben 2025 (DE):** Explicitly acknowledges crypto tax software as legitimate documentation.

## Strategic Conclusion

US-first (larger, monthly billing whitespace, 1099-DA urgency), DACH-second (strong #2 market, privacy positioning, underserved). Build country module interface from day one.
